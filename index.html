Running a Triangulation Fix

How Triangulation Works
Enter at least 3 GPS positions and compass bearings from different vantage points. Pinpoint finds the point where those bearing lines best intersect — your estimated target location.
Step 1 — Configure Settings
Set your magnetic declination for your region (use the Look Up link to find it). Set your bearing error to match your compass accuracy — a lower value assumes a more precise instrument.
Step 2 — Add Observers
Each observer card represents one vantage point. Enter the GPS coordinates of that position and the compass bearing toward the signal source. Use the GPS button on each card to auto-fill your current location.
Step 3 — Read the Results
Results calculate automatically as you type — no button to press. The results card shows the estimated target coordinates in both decimal and DMS format, plus an error radius. The diagram below shows your bearing lines, estimated target, and error circle on the map.
Tips for a Better Fix
Spread your observers around the target — observers clustered together or bearing lines that run nearly parallel produce a weaker fix. The GDOP warning will alert you if your geometry is too weak to produce a reliable result. More observers generally means a tighter error radius.

Learn More

Algorithms
The algorithm selector changes the math used to compute the fix. Least Squares (OLS) is the default and works well in most situations. Premium algorithms like Maximum Likelihood and the M-Estimators are better when one of your bearings might be significantly off — they reduce the influence of outlier observations.
Toggling Observations
Use the toggle on each observer card to temporarily disable an observation without deleting it. Useful for testing how much a single bearing is affecting your result. Toggles are only available when you have more than 3 active observations — adding more than 3 requires a premium account.
Dark Mode
The theme button in the top right switches to a high-contrast light mode designed for outdoor use in bright conditions.
Saving Your Session
Your inputs are saved automatically as you type and restored when you reopen the tool. Use the Clear / Reset button to start a new session from scratch.
Your Account
Access account settings from the menu — manage your registered devices or change your password at any time.
Devices
Premium accounts can be used on up to 2 devices simultaneously. Each time you sign in on a new device it is registered to your account. If you sign in on a third device, the least recently used device is automatically removed. You can view and manage your registered devices at any time from Manage Account in the menu. If you remove the device you are currently using, you will be signed out automatically.
